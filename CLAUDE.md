# InterSacks Apparel — instructions for Claude

## Project
- InterSacks Apparel storefront: React + Vite multi-page site (index, product, cart, custom).
- Deploys to GitHub Pages on every push to `main` (`.github/workflows/pages.yml`).
- Build with `npm run build`; test with `npm test`.

## Standing rules from the owner
1. **Business details, not personal ones.** Whenever details on the site, in marketing or in documents are changed (names, email, phone, WhatsApp, socials, addresses), switch personal details over to the InterSacks Apparel business details. If a business detail isn't known yet, ask for it instead of reusing a personal one.
2. **Don't show the real 3D model.** In new work, keep the 3D tee model out of the website and out of promo videos and other marketing. Use the product photos, tee mockups and lookbook views instead. The existing site and the first promo video stay as they are unless the owner asks for a change.
3. **Preview, then merge.** Open a pull request and share a preview first. Merge only after the owner says "merge it", then confirm the GitHub Pages deploy.
